Our Story | More Than Just a Coffee Shop

Our story started with a simple idea: a coffee shop made for people who love furry friends. The owner wondered, what if the Philippines had a place where pet lovers could rent adorable furry pets too?

Because if you need someone to accompany you, or want to treat yourself to a reward with our furry friends, why not?

That idea became The Pawfee Shop, a cozy, realistic coffee shop where you can rent a furry friend per hour and enjoy your coffee while spending quality time with them.
